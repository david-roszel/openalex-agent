# Evaluation and research status

The release contains engineering infrastructure, small synthetic/authentic metadata fixtures, and qualified historical aggregate observations. None establishes that graph memory or CLM improves retrieval. See RESULTS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md; raw private empirical artifacts are not distributed.

Separate diagnostics, development protocol testing and final research evaluation.
Use independently judged, adequately sourced data for empirical conclusions.
Reserve a final question set privately; freeze the protocol before final runs.
A target study can use approximately 10–20 held-out questions and at least three
repetitions, but no such completed evaluation is asserted here.

All factorial cells use matched candidate corpus, questions, provider settings,
action/inspection/context/inference limits and trial identities. Graph and
working-context interventions remain different by design. Retain counterbalanced
schedules, failures, fallbacks and missing outcomes. Analyze paired questions and
within-question variability rather than treating decisions as independent samples.

Precision/nDCG require relevance grades with known coverage. Unjudged papers
remain unknown. Factual-answer accuracy, answer coverage, grounded completion,
support, unsupported claims and contradictions answer different questions.
Source identity or accessible text does not by itself verify a claim. Independent
human judgments and evidence locations are required for semantic conclusions.

`development_review.prepare` creates blank worksheets from a supplied corpus,
public question file, factual-probe file and preliminary candidate file. Supply
`--questions`, `--factual-questions`, `--candidates` and a private `--reserved`
list for your own protocol. Bundled defaults are synthetic regression examples.
Candidate adequacy is preliminary, not independently verified truth. The existing
review validator and dashboard approval gates remain in place. Paid consent or
a generated ready flag cannot substitute for completed review.

Final dashboard execution requires a reviewed matching question/corpus bundle,
matching imported human labels, source/config/provider pins, at least three
trials, 10–20 held-out questions and holdout-independence attestation. Software
can validate structures/attestations, not prove a person's independence.
Lower-level CLI access remains a trusted research interface, not automatic
scientific approval. Synthetic examples must never be promoted into final data.
