# What the implementation and existing results establish

This update releases code, small examples, frozen research source and a qualified aggregate status summary. It runs no new hosted experiment. Machine-readable counts are in [audited-development-summary.json](../examples/research-status/audited-development-summary.json), with checksums of the archived audit documents used. Raw private experimental outputs/corpus/keys are not redistributed: the aggregates alone do not permit independent reproduction of empirical contrasts. Public mock tests reproduce engineering behavior only.

## Historical secondary retrieval cohort

Thirty constructed development tasks ×five methods ×three repetitions yield450 terminal trajectories:442 completed/eight failed. Mean known-target Recall@10 is .333333 standard, .414815 llm, .422222 llm_graph, .422222 clm, .392593 clm_graph. These are local frozen lexical retrieval outcomes with a single-pass, unequal-work reference, not live OpenAlex or independent general relevance judgments.

The historical planned horizon10 primary comparison remains incomplete. Historical graph-arm inputs lacked complete explicit directed citation subgraphs, although graph-derived proposals/ranking/reference arrays existed. Missing factual final answers were concentrated in context-managed arms. Neither graph nor CLM established a benefit, equivalence, generalization or scientifically complete answer generation. Do not relabel the completed secondary cohort as the completed main study.

## Measurement boundaries

- Transport success, terminal status, retrieval success, evidence delivery, valid final format and independently supported scientific completion are different outcomes.
- Known-target recall is not general relevance. Source membership/quotation matching is not entailment, truth, contradiction assessment or completeness. Human grounding/relevance judgments remain NOT EVALUATED.
- Stored edges are not delivered edges. Delivered information is not proof of causal model use. Endpoint co-discovery is not automatically observed directed-edge evidence.
- Repetitions, phases, papers, branches and requests are not independent research tasks. Missing/unrun cells are not measured zeros; attempted failures retain their protocol-specific operational handling.
- Context editing/recovery consumes resources. Equal ceilings do not guarantee equal exposure or compute. Synthesis reload can hide an active-context retention failure.
- Runtime/tokens/cost are conditional on work/settings and configured prices. They do not establish cost-effectiveness superiority; conservative retained exposure is not an invoice or verified credit balance.

The defensible contribution is an instrumented scholarly agent plus an empirical investigation of evidence accessibility, context management and reliability. Official Meta CLM stays exploratory. No final judged held-out scientific evaluation, hallucination reduction or graph/CLM advantage is claimed.
